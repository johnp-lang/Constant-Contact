Why Uniform, specifically over Contentful

Five reasons to move off Contentful now

01

Everything else in your stack stays exactly as it is

Uniform becomes the system of record for content, replacing Contentful outright. What doesn't change: Vercel hosting, the React/Next.js front-end, and Tealium as the CDP all stay exactly as they are.

Contentful was acquired by Salesforce in June 2026. Its roadmap now answers to Salesforce's priorities, not just its own customers' — one more reason this is the right moment to move off it rather than build further on top of it.

02

Personalization built in, not bolted on

Rule-based targeting by vertical, geography, device, and audience list runs natively at the edge, reading straight from Tealium — the exact requirement Klodi sent over.

Contentful has no native personalization layer; it typically requires a third-party add-on to do what you're asking for out of the box.

03

Content management and page composition, in one system

Once migrated, Uniform holds the content and gives marketing a visual workspace to build and edit pages directly — the shift from "engineering ships every page" to "marketing ships in hours," with nothing left behind in Contentful.

Contentful stores structured content well, but someone still has to turn that content into a page. Today, that someone is your front-of-site team — and it stays that way as long as Contentful stays in place.

04

A migration path that starts where you already are

Siphon extracts your existing structured entries into a neutral format, and the Contentful integration allows a phased cutover instead of a hard deadline.

Contentful's own structured content model is actually one of the more migration-friendly ones we see — this isn't a rebuild-from-scratch project.

05

Built for AI-assisted creation, not retrofitted for it

Uniform Code generates components and pages from screenshots or Figma, aligned to your brand assets, with structured content underneath that's already legible to AI tooling — directly supporting the AI enablement initiative driving this evaluation.

Contentful's content model wasn't built with agentic or AI-assisted authoring in mind; that layer gets bolted on rather than designed in from the start.
